# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Memoryling a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act in ways that support an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas and work, not people.
- Respect privacy and do not share another person's private information.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, trolling, sustained disruption, and publication of private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Repository maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors.

This document is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
